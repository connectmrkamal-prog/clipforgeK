How it works

  1. Paste a link or upload a video.
  2. ClipForge transcribes it and asks the AI which moments will hold attention.
  3. Review the captioned 9:16 clips, then download or post them.

Questions

Which videos can I clip?

Only videos you made or have permission to use. You are responsible for the rights to what you download, clip and post.

Do I need an AI key?

No. Without one, clips are picked by loudness. With a key, the AI picks better moments and writes titles.

Does my audio leave my machine?

Speech to text runs on the server you run ClipForge on. Only the transcript text goes to the AI service you choose.
